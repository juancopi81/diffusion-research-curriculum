Faisal, and Cheng Soon Ong. The book was published by Cambridge University
Press in 2020.

## Phase 2 Reading Map

### Week 9: Linear Algebra and PCA

The linear algebra refresh uses these selected sections:

- Chapter 2, §§2.2–2.7: matrices, vector spaces, linear independence, basis,
  rank, and linear mappings
- Chapter 3, §§3.1–3.6 and §3.8: norms, inner products, orthogonality,
  orthonormal bases, and projections
- Chapter 4, §§4.2, 4.4–4.6: eigenvalues, eigendecomposition, SVD, and matrix
  approximation
- Chapter 10, §§10.1–10.4: targeted reference for the PCA notebook, not an
  additional full reading assignment

### Week 10: Multivariate Gaussians and Scores

- Chapter 5, §§5.2 and 5.5: gradients and useful gradient identities
- Chapter 6, §6.5, especially §§6.5.1, 6.5.3, and 6.5.4: Gaussian
  conditionals, linear transformations, and sampling

### Week 12: Normalizing Flows

- Chapter 5, §5.3: Jacobians of vector-valued functions
- Chapter 6, §6.7: change of variables and inverse transforms

### Week 13: Optimization

- Chapter 7, §7.1 only: gradient descent, step size, momentum, and stochastic
  gradient descent
- Adam is not covered by the book and requires a separate source
