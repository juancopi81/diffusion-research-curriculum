# Stat 110 Strategic Practice and Homework

Original course materials by Prof. Joe Blitzstein, Department of Statistics,
Harvard University. The documents contain strategic practice problems and
solutions, a homework assignment, and homework solutions.

- Official collection: [Strategic Practice and Homework Problems](https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/strategic-practice-problems)
- Document date: Fall 2011
- Local study copies: [`pdfs/`](./pdfs/)
- Redistribution status: no Creative Commons or other redistribution license
  was found on the collection page or in the documents during the July 10,
  2026 review. The PDF files are therefore gitignored and must not be committed
  unless permission or a compatible license is documented here.
